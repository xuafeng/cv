# About

I am a senior researcher at [Microsoft Research Asia - Vancouver Lab](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/group/microsoft-research-asia-vancouver/) working on future agent systems, system intelligence, and AI for operational and defensive security.

Previously, I was a security researcher in the [Trustworthy Systems group](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/group/trustworthy-systems-asia/) at [Microsoft Research Asia - Beijing](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/lab/microsoft-research-asia/).

I received my Ph.D. from the [Institute of Information Engineering](http://www.iie.ac.cn/), Chinese Academy of Sciences in 2020, and visited [Indiana University Bloomington](https://www.indiana.edu/) as a visiting scholar in 2018.

You can reach me at [xuafeng@microsoft.com](mailto:xuafeng@microsoft.com).
